Use cases

Every portal workflow your team runs today can become one Aevra execution.

Shipment booking

Book across carriers without switching tabs.

Whether your team books through DHL, DB Schenker, Kuehne+Nagel, or five other carriers, Aevra handles each portal's flow automatically. Same data, right fields, every booking — no manual re-entry.

Customs filing

File customs once. Not once per portal.

Customs declarations, HS code lookups, and compliance forms require the same data entered into five different government and carrier systems. Aevra captures the full filing sequence and executes it with zero re-keying.

Status tracking

Pull shipment status from every portal into one place.

Your team shouldn't spend 40 minutes a day logging into eight carrier portals to check where shipments are. Aevra runs the status check workflow across all of them and surfaces the result where your team works.

Freight documentation

Generate bills of lading, CMRs, and AWBs without the busywork.

Freight documentation follows fixed rules. Aevra captures how your team fills each template, pulls the right data from the right system, and produces the document correctly — without manual field-by-field entry.

Ops onboarding

New operators booking shipments on their first day.

Learning 8 different portals takes months. Aevra gives new ops team members step-by-step guidance through each portal workflow as they execute it, so they can start contributing before they have every system memorized.
