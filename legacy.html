How it works

Three steps. From manual task to automated workflow.

Step 01 — Record

Your ops team performs the task once, normally.

An operator logs into your ERP, claim portal, or billing system and completes the task exactly as they always do. Aevra runs in the background, capturing not just the clicks but the intent behind them.

No scripts to write. No configuration to set up. No technical knowledge needed at this stage or any other.

Average recording time: under 5 minutes

Step 02 — Automate

Aevra runs that workflow on every new case.

One demonstration covers every future case — different inputs, different data, different outcomes. Aevra executes the workflow the way your best operator would: correctly, completely, every time.

Tasks that took minutes per case now run in seconds. Volume your team could never absorb gets handled automatically.

Execution speed: seconds per case

Step 03 — Own it

Your ops team manages, updates, and scales.

Workflows are owned by your operations team, not your IT department. When a process changes, your ops manager updates it — no developer needed, no support ticket, no waiting.

Every workflow becomes a permanent company asset. When someone who knew that process leaves, the knowledge stays.

Update time when process changes: minutes
